🚀 Section A: Command-Line & Secure Analytical Tools (Newest First)

**Fast phylogenetic GLMMs in glmmTMB (new `propto` covariance structure)** (Release Date: 2025-12-23)
Links: [GitHub](https://github.com/glmmTMB/glmmTMB) | [Preprint](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2025.12.20.695312)
Function: Adds a phylogenetic covariance structure (`propto`) to the general-purpose R package glmmTMB, letting it fit phylogenetic generalised linear mixed models (PGLMMs) with repeated measures and non-Gaussian responses. Benchmarked against brms, MCMCglmm, INLA and phyr — faster overall with comparable estimates.
Use Case: Any comparative-method analysis needing to separate phylogenetic signal from other effects (trait evolution, host-pathogen trait correlations, AMR phenotype vs. phylogeny) without switching to a slower Bayesian-only tool.
Usability Note: glmmTMB itself is a mature, widely-used CRAN package (actively maintained, responsive maintainers on GitHub issues); this is a new feature in an existing stable tool rather than a new standalone project — low adoption risk.
Tags: #phylogenetics #r-lang

**ProFam** (Release Date: 2025-12-21)
Links: [GitHub](https://github.com/alex-hh/profam) | [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/profam/) | [Preprint](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2025.12.19.695431)
Function: Open-source suite (data, weights, training/inference code) for autoregressive protein-family language models (pfLMs) — ProFam-1 is a 251M-parameter Llama-architecture transformer trained on the "ProFam Atlas" (~40M protein families, ~481M sequences), used for sequence scoring, fitness prediction and conditional generation.
Use Case: A fully open alternative to closed/weights-only protein language models for functional annotation and variant-effect/fitness prediction tasks — relevant wherever AMR-phenotype-from-sequence or structural pathogen-characterization pipelines currently rely on a pLM (focus area 2).
Usability Note: MIT license, 1650 commits, 57 stars, 15 forks, 9 open issues with active PRs — looks actively developed; too new to judge maintainer responsiveness on user-filed issues specifically.
Tags: #plm #python

**STUBENTIGER** (Release Date: 2025-12-21)
Links: [GitHub](https://github.com/bacpop/Stubentiger) | [Preprint](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2025.12.18.695090)
Function: R package implementing a reusable Wright-Fisher-based model of pangenome/accessory-genome selection (negative frequency-dependent selection) to explain and forecast *Streptococcus pneumoniae* serotype replacement after conjugate-vaccine introduction, and to evaluate cost-effective genomic surveillance sampling strategies (sample size vs. sampling frequency).
Use Case: Designing or justifying a genomic surveillance sampling scheme for a vaccine-preventable pathogen — directly applicable to any PCV-type vaccine rollout being tracked genomically, and a template for similar accessory-genome selection questions in other bacteria.
Usability Note: Apache-2.0 license, README with install/usage examples, 36 commits, 1 open issue (from bacpop/Lees lab, an established genomic-epidemiology group) — too early in this window to judge issue responsiveness.
Tags: #amr #surveillance-ready #r-lang

**AFS-MetaCache2** (Release Date: 2025-12-21)
Links: [GitHub](https://github.com/muellan/metacache) | [Preprint](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2025.12.18.694891)
Function: Extends the MetaCache metagenomic classifier with an improved reference-database construction method for All-Food-Sequencing (AFS) — untargeted detection/quantification of food ingredients (animal, plant, microbial) from total genomic DNA. This release benchmarks Illumina vs. Nanopore input and shows long-read sequencing gives better quantification accuracy and lower false-positive rates than Kraken2+Bracken, KrakenUniq and AFS-MetaCache1, with faster database builds and lower memory.
Use Case: Food authentication / adulteration surveillance and bio-surveillance of food ingredients — a metagenomics application directly relevant to food security (focus area 9) rather than clinical/environmental metagenomics.
Usability Note: Free-software license (GPL-family, per repo footer), comprehensive README, 1,039 commits, 63 stars, 11 open issues — development clearly active; issue-answering responsiveness for outside users not confirmed this cycle.
Tags: #metagenomics #one-health

💡 Methodological Trend

- **Reinforcement learning as a framework for modelling AMR dynamics, not just detecting it.** A preprint this cycle frames repeated antimicrobial (copper) exposure as a sequential decision problem and compares random, rule-based and tabular Q-learning agents for keeping resistance burden down under exposure constraints. No released tool and explicitly not claimed as predictive/mechanistic — but worth watching as an early example of control-theory/RL framing being applied to AMR management decisions rather than only to AMR prediction from sequence (focus area 10).
- **Single-cell genomics is starting to resolve mobile-genetic-element host ranges directly, in situ.** A ~60,000-single-cell-genome survey (gut and environmental microbiomes) links individual MGEs (plasmids, phage, ARG-carrying elements) to their actual host cells rather than inferring host range computationally — finding both narrow, species-specific MGEs and some that recur, nearly identical, across distant host phyla. No standalone software tool is released, but the SAG-based approach is a methodological alternative to metagenomic co-occurrence/network inference for tracking ARG exchange (focus areas 5 and 8).
