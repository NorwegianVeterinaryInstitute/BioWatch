💡 Methodological Trend

Foundation-model momentum continues to spread from protein language models
into other sequence types. Two bioRxiv preprints landed squarely in this
window:

- **LIMBER** (posted 2026-10-28) — a hybrid RNA foundation model for
  predicting and understanding RNA structure and function.
  Link: https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.28.762670v1
  Why it's worth flagging under focus area 10: RNA-level foundation models
  (as opposed to protein pLMs like ESM-2) are the logical next step for
  RNA virus work — segment reassortment, structural constraint analysis,
  functional annotation of viral ncRNA — relevant to influenza/H5N1-type
  surveillance even though this preprint itself is not pathogen-focused.
  Code availability and license could not be confirmed this cycle.
- **TriStageHLA** (posted 2026-10-29) — a three-stage high-throughput
  screening framework for peptide–HLA binding and immunogenicity.
  Link: https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.29.762831v1
  Human-immunology-focused, but the staged screening architecture
  (presentation → binding → immunogenicity) is a transferable pattern for
  any multi-stage ML prediction pipeline, including AMR phenotype-from-
  sequence work. Code availability and license could not be confirmed
  this cycle.
